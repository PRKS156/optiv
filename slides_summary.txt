  Strategic Context: The Silent AI Proliferation in Banking


  The Meridian Challenge

  • Core Banking Dependency: Meridian relies on an extensive vendor ecosystem across core ledgers, real-time payments, reporting, and print operations.
  • Silent AI Adoption: Vendors are rapidly embedding generative AI and autonomous agents into production workflows without formal disclosure to client risk teams.
  • TPRM Governance Blindspot: Traditional vendor questionnaires are subjective, point-in-time, and fail to capture rapid continuous AI updates.
  • Critical Downstream Risks: Unvetted exposure of customer PII to LLMs, reliance on undisclosed sub-processors (OpenAI, Microsoft), and black-box decision models.


  The Optiv Solution Framework

  ✔ Objective Public Footprint (OSINT): Examine verifiable technical and regulatory public footprints to uncover real commercial AI capabilities rather than PR commentary.
  ✔ Deterministic Criticality Scoring: A repeatable 4-factor formula ensures any analyst assigns identical criticality tiers based on data sensitivity and operational dependency.
  ✔ Tier-Driven Assessment Depth: Assessment depth is governed strictly by vendor criticality tier, maximizing analytical ROI and eliminating wasted investigative effort.
  ✔ Strict Source Traceability: Verbatim evidence excerpts, cryptographic/timestamped sources, and transparent evidentiary weights keep findings fully auditable.

  Vendor Criticality: 100-Point Deterministic Framework


  Objective 4-Factor Rubric

  1. Data Sensitivity Accessed (Max 30 pts)
  30 = Full customer master/balances/tax IDs; 20 = System credentials/config or wealth client data; 15 = Payment messages in transit; 0 = Public.
  2. Operational Dependency (Max 30 pts)
  30 = Critical (core operations halt immediately); 20 = High (daily clearing/batch degraded); 10 = Moderate (monthly cycles withstand delay).
  3. Payment Flow & Rail Involvement (Max 20 pts)
  20 = Systemic national clearing rail (ACH/RTP); 15 = Live core transactional ledger or real-time payment gateway; 5 = Batch scheduling.
  4. Volume & Regulatory Scrutiny (Max 20 pts)
  20 = >100M txns, bank-wide scale; 15 = >10M msgs/docs or IRS tax filings; 10 = Millions of msgs; 5 = Monthly wealth batches.


  Criticality-Driven Assessment Depth

  CRITICAL TIER (Score 80–100) -> Forensic Review
  Full OSINT sweep across trust portals, sub-processor registers, SEC 10-K filings, API developer docs, executive channels, plus mandatory vendor questionnaire.
  HIGH TIER (Score 60–79) -> Targeted Technical Review
  Investigate service line architecture, engineering job listings, trust center certifications, and targeted AI addendum in contracts.
  MODERATE TIER (Score 40–59) -> Standard OSINT Verification
  Primary web domain, product specification sheets, privacy disclosures, and blog/thought-leadership cross-checking for production reality.
  LOW TIER (Score <40) -> Surface Scan
  Standard baseline review of commercial terms and public trust overview. Avoid wasteful over-investigation.

  Source Strategy: Evidentiary Weighting & False-Positive Filtering


  Tier 1

  Weight: 0.90 – 1.00
  • Official Trust Centers & Sub-processor Registers
  • SEC Filings (10-K, 10-Q Item 1A Risk Disclosures)
  • Technical API Documentation, SDK Guides & Release Notes
  • SOC 2 Type II System Description & ISO 27001 Annexes


  Tier 2

  Weight: 0.70 – 0.85
  • Technical Job Postings (Applied AI / LLM Orchestration)
  • Public Developer Repositories, PyPI/npm Packages & SDKs
  • Technical Engineering Whitepapers & Patent Filings
  • Developer Community Q&A (StackOverflow, GitHub Issues)


  Tier 3

  Weight: 0.50 – 0.65
  • C-Suite Verified Announcements (e.g. CEO LinkedIn Posts)
  • Official Corporate Press Releases announcing AI partnerships
  • Commercial Product Launch Webinars & Keynote Briefings
  • Requires cross-referencing with Tier 1/2 for technical confirmation


  Tier 4

  Weight: 0.10 – 0.30 (Discounted)
  • General Blog Posts discussing 'The Future of AI in Finance'
  • Sales Collateral claiming 'AI-Powered Insights' without architecture
  • Educational Articles on emerging technology trends
  • RULE: Treated as Inconclusive/Negative for production AI risk
