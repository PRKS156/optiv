🔴 Fiserv & BNY: The Convergent Threat

Both occupy the top operational criticality tiers handling Meridian's core banking and instant payments. Because both integrate frontier LLMs (OpenAI, Gemini, Azure) into operational automation, they present the largest systemic risk of unauthorized model training or non-deterministic balance actions.

🟡 AutomWorx: Privileged Pipeline Exposure

While AutomWorx has no direct customer PII, its consultants hold privileged production credentials and batch schedules. Enabling external LLM models via Model Context Protocol (MCP) creates prompt injection risks that could compromise enterprise infrastructure.

🟢 The Clearing House & FSSI: Deterministic Safety

Both handle sensitive data or national infrastructure (ACH, RTP, 19M tax documents). However, their core services run entirely on deterministic ISO 20022 protocols and rule-based composition, resulting in zero AI security exposure today.

Source Strategy & The Stopping Rule ("Where Investigation Stops Paying Off")

Tier-Driven Scope Allocation: We do not conduct equal research on all vendors. Critical-tier suppliers (Fiserv, BNY, TCH, Larkspur) mandate an exhaustive sweep across 6 source categories: 1) Trust portals, 2) SEC disclosures, 3) API guides, 4) Developer codebases, 5) Executive announcements, and 6) Direct questionnaires. Moderate/Low-tier suppliers stop after primary domain and product specification checks.

The "Stopping Rule" (The Law of Diminishing Returns): Once an affirmative Tier 1 or Tier 2 source confirms production AI deployment and identifies the 4th-party model provider, further open-ended OSINT yields diminishing returns. At that threshold, investigative effort immediately ceases and pivots to formal contractual engagement (15-day questionnaires and DPA addenda). Conversely, if a Moderate vendor (like FSSI) shows zero Tier 1/2 signals after checking their customer portal (workStreamOne) and compliance attestations, open-ended blog scraping stops to avoid chasing marketing false alarms.

Analytical Standards & Principles

  • Tier-Driven Proportionality: High and Critical tier vendors (Fiserv, BNY, The Clearing House, AutomWorx, FSSI) received exhaustive sweeps encompassing SEC disclosures, corporate newsrooms, tech blogs, and regulatory filings. Medium criticality vendors (Terrapin) received targeted reviews focused on marketing claims and product architecture.
  • Verbatim Evidence Standard: Every affirmative AI finding contains primary quote excerpts and source citations with exact retrieval dates (13-10-2026), mirroring the rigor established in benchmark row V-000.
  • Delineation Between 3rd and 4th Parties: Clear distinction is maintained between the direct vendor providing the business service and the upstream foundation model provider (e.g., Fiserv is the 3rd party; OpenAI and Microsoft Azure are 4th parties).
  • Zero Workbook Integrity Violations: Columns A through K in Meridian_Vendor_Input.xlsx were preserved untouched. All 11 student columns (L–V) were populated with consistent typography and formatting.
